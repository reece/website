I am an engineering leader and scientist. I have over two decades of experience architecting, building, and operating reliable software systems for research, healthcare, and business in large companies ([IBM](https://ibm.com/), [Genentech](https://gene.com/)), zero-to-one startups ([Invitae](https://invitae.com/), [Genome Medical](https://genomemedical.com/), [MyOme](https://myome.com/)), and open source collaborations ([biocommons.org](https://biocommons.org/), [GA4GH](https://ga4gh.org/)). Throughout my career, I've worked to balance product, scientific, and technical depth while building kind, enjoyable, focused, and effective teams. I thrive in collaborating with smart people to build teams and systems that create financial and human value. I am a strategic thinker first, which I balance with a bias toward action and incremental value creation.  I remain actively involved in many of these efforts and seek additional part-time engagements in scientific computing.

In addition to the above, I have increasingly focused my time on improving civic engagement across the political spectrum by volunteering for and contracting with mission-aligned organizations.
